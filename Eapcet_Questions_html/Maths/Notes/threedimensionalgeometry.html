Three Dimensional Geometry

Coordinate System, Distance & Section Formulae

3D space is divided into 8 octants by three mutually perpendicular coordinate axes (X, Y, Z). The origin is \(O(0,0,0)\).

Distance & Section Formulae
  • Distance between \(P(x_1,y_1,z_1)\) and \(Q(x_2,y_2,z_2)\): \[PQ = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2+(z_2-z_1)^2}\]
  • Internal division in ratio \(m:n\): \[\left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\;\frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n},\;\frac{mz_2+nz_1}{m+n}\right)\]
  • External division in ratio \(m:n\): \[\left(\frac{mx_2-nx_1}{m-n},\;\frac{my_2-ny_1}{m-n},\;\frac{mz_2-nz_1}{m-n}\right)\]
  • Mid-point: \(\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2},\;\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2},\;\dfrac{z_1+z_2}{2}\right)\)
  • Centroid of triangle with vertices \((x_i,y_i,z_i)\): \[\left(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3},\;\frac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3},\;\frac{z_1+z_2+z_3}{3}\right)\]
  • Centroid of tetrahedron with vertices \((x_i,y_i,z_i)\), \(i=1,2,3,4\): \[\left(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4}{4},\;\frac{y_1+y_2+y_3+y_4}{4},\;\frac{z_1+z_2+z_3+z_4}{4}\right)\]
  • Area of triangle with vertices \(A,B,C\): \[\Delta = \sqrt{\Delta_{yz}^2 + \Delta_{zx}^2 + \Delta_{xy}^2}\] where \(\Delta_{yz} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{vmatrix}y_1&z_1&1\\y_2&z_2&1\\y_3&z_3&1\end{vmatrix}\), etc.

Direction Cosines & Direction Ratios

If a directed line OP makes angles \(\alpha, \beta, \gamma\) with OX, OY, OZ respectively, then \(l = \cos\alpha\), \(m = \cos\beta\), \(n = \cos\gamma\) are the direction cosines (DCs).

  • \(l^2 + m^2 + n^2 = 1\)
  • If \(|\vec{r}| = r\) and DCs are \(l,m,n\): \(\vec{r} = r(l\hat{i}+m\hat{j}+n\hat{k})\); coordinates of P are \((lr, mr, nr)\).
  • DCs of \(\overrightarrow{PQ}\): \(l = \dfrac{x_2-x_1}{|PQ|}\), \(m = \dfrac{y_2-y_1}{|PQ|}\), \(n = \dfrac{z_2-z_1}{|PQ|}\).

Direction Ratios (DRs): Numbers \(a, b, c\) proportional to \(l, m, n\). Then: \[l = \pm\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}},\quad m = \pm\frac{b}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}},\quad n = \pm\frac{c}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}\]

Angle Between Two Lines

With DCs \((l_1,m_1,n_1)\) and \((l_2,m_2,n_2)\):

\[\cos\theta = l_1l_2 + m_1m_2 + n_1n_2\]
  • Parallel: \(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2} = \dfrac{m_1}{m_2} = \dfrac{n_1}{n_2}\)  (i.e. \(a_1/a_2 = b_1/b_2 = c_1/c_2\) for DRs)
  • Perpendicular: \(l_1l_2+m_1m_2+n_1n_2 = 0\)  (i.e. \(a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2=0\) for DRs)

With DRs \((a_1,b_1,c_1)\) and \((a_2,b_2,c_2)\): \[\cos\theta = \frac{a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2+c_1^2}\,\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2+c_2^2}}\]

Projection of \(PQ\) on line with DCs \(l,m,n\): \[|(x_2-x_1)l+(y_2-y_1)m+(z_2-z_1)n|\]

  • Angle between diagonals of a cube: \(\cos^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\)
  • Angle between a diagonal of cube and face diagonal: \(\cos^{-1}\!\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}}\right)\)

Lines in Space

Equations of a Line

Through point \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) with DRs \(a,b,c\):

Vector: \(\vec{r} = \vec{a} + \lambda\vec{b}\)  |  Cartesian (symmetric form): \[\frac{x-x_1}{a} = \frac{y-y_1}{b} = \frac{z-z_1}{c}\]

Through two points \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2,z_2)\):

Vector: \(\vec{r} = \vec{a} + \lambda(\vec{b}-\vec{a})\)  |  Cartesian: \[\frac{x-x_1}{x_2-x_1} = \frac{y-y_1}{y_2-y_1} = \frac{z-z_1}{z_2-z_1}\]

Equations of axes: X-axis: \(y=0, z=0\); Y-axis: \(x=0, z=0\); Z-axis: \(x=0, y=0\).

Perpendicular Distance of a Point from a Line

Vector form: Distance from \(P(\vec{\alpha})\) to line \(\vec{r} = \vec{a} + \lambda\vec{b}\): \[d = \sqrt{|\vec{\alpha}-\vec{a}|^2 - \frac{[(\vec{\alpha}-\vec{a})\cdot\vec{b}]^2}{|\vec{b}|^2}}\]

Skew Lines & Shortest Distance

Skew lines: Non-parallel, non-intersecting lines in space.

Shortest distance (SD) — Vector form between \(\vec{r}=\vec{a}_1+\lambda\vec{b}_1\) and \(\vec{r}=\vec{a}_2+\mu\vec{b}_2\):

\[d = \frac{|(\vec{a}_2-\vec{a}_1)\cdot(\vec{b}_1\times\vec{b}_2)|}{|\vec{b}_1\times\vec{b}_2|}\]

Cartesian form:

\[d = \frac{\begin{vmatrix}x_2-x_1&y_2-y_1&z_2-z_1\\a_1&b_1&c_1\\a_2&b_2&c_2\end{vmatrix}}{\sqrt{(a_1b_2-a_2b_1)^2+(b_1c_2-b_2c_1)^2+(c_1a_2-c_2a_1)^2}}\]

SD between parallel lines \(\vec{r}=\vec{a}_1+\lambda\vec{b}\) and \(\vec{r}=\vec{a}_2+\mu\vec{b}\): \[d = \frac{|(\vec{a}_2-\vec{a}_1)\times\vec{b}|}{|\vec{b}|}\]

Lines intersect (coplanar) iff \((\vec{a}_2-\vec{a}_1)\cdot(\vec{b}_1\times\vec{b}_2) = 0\), i.e. SD = 0.

Planes

General equation: \(ax + by + cz + d = 0\). Coefficients \((a,b,c)\) are the DRs of the normal to the plane.

Forms of Equation of a Plane
  • Normal form: \(lx + my + nz = p\) (l,m,n are DCs of normal; p = distance from origin)
  • Vector normal form: \(\vec{r}\cdot\hat{n} = d\)
  • Through point \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) with normal \((a,b,c)\): \[a(x-x_1)+b(y-y_1)+c(z-z_1)=0\]
  • Intercept form: \(\dfrac{x}{a}+\dfrac{y}{b}+\dfrac{z}{c}=1\) (intercepts \(a,b,c\) on axes)
  • Through three non-collinear points \((x_i,y_i,z_i)\): \[\begin{vmatrix}x-x_1&y-y_1&z-z_1\\x_2-x_1&y_2-y_1&z_2-z_1\\x_3-x_1&y_3-y_1&z_3-z_1\end{vmatrix}=0\]
  • Family through intersection of \(\pi_1\) and \(\pi_2\): \(\pi_1 + \lambda\pi_2 = 0\)
  • Parallel to given plane \(ax+by+cz+d_1=0\): \(ax+by+cz+d_2=0\)
Angles, Distance & Relationships

Angle between two planes \(a_1x+b_1y+c_1z+d_1=0\) and \(a_2x+b_2y+c_2z+d_2=0\):

\[\cos\theta = \frac{|a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2|}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2+c_1^2}\,\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2+c_2^2}}\]
  • Parallel: \(\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}=\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}=\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}\)
  • Perpendicular: \(a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2=0\)

Distance of point \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) from plane \(ax+by+cz+d=0\): \[d = \frac{|ax_1+by_1+cz_1+d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}\]

Distance between parallel planes \(ax+by+cz+d_1=0\) and \(ax+by+cz+d_2=0\): \[d = \frac{|d_2-d_1|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}\]

Angle between line \(\dfrac{x-x_1}{a_1}=\dfrac{y-y_1}{b_1}=\dfrac{z-z_1}{c_1}\) and plane \(a_2x+b_2y+c_2z+d_2=0\): \[\sin\phi = \frac{|a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2|}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2+c_1^2}\,\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2+c_2^2}}\]

Image, Foot & Coplanarity

Image of \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) in plane \(ax+by+cz+d=0\): \[\frac{x-x_1}{a} = \frac{y-y_1}{b} = \frac{z-z_1}{c} = \frac{-2(ax_1+by_1+cz_1+d)}{a^2+b^2+c^2}\]

Foot of perpendicular from \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) to plane: \[\frac{x-x_1}{a} = \frac{y-y_1}{b} = \frac{z-z_1}{c} = \frac{-(ax_1+by_1+cz_1+d)}{a^2+b^2+c^2}\]

Four points coplanar iff: \[\begin{vmatrix}x_2-x_1&y_2-y_1&z_2-z_1\\x_3-x_1&y_3-y_1&z_3-z_1\\x_4-x_1&y_4-y_1&z_4-z_1\end{vmatrix}=0\]

Bisector planes of \(a_1x+b_1y+c_1z+d_1=0\) and \(a_2x+b_2y+c_2z+d_2=0\): \[\frac{a_1x+b_1y+c_1z+d_1}{\sqrt{\sum a_1^2}} = \pm\frac{a_2x+b_2y+c_2z+d_2}{\sqrt{\sum a_2^2}}\]

Sphere

A sphere is the locus of a point at constant distance (radius) from a fixed point (centre).

Equations of a Sphere
  • Standard form, centre \((a,b,c)\), radius \(r\): \[(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2+(z-c)^2=r^2\]
  • General form: \(x^2+y^2+z^2+2ux+2vy+2wz+d=0\)
    • Centre \(= (-u,-v,-w)\)
    • Radius \(= \sqrt{u^2+v^2+w^2-d}\)
  • Sphere on diameter \((x_1,y_1,z_1)\) to \((x_2,y_2,z_2)\): \[(x-x_1)(x-x_2)+(y-y_1)(y-y_2)+(z-z_1)(z-z_2)=0\]
Conditions & Important Results
  • General second-degree equation represents a sphere iff \(a = b = c\) (coefficients of \(x^2,y^2,z^2\) are equal) and \(h = k = l = 0\) (no \(xy, yz, zx\) terms).
  • Sphere is real iff \(u^2+v^2+w^2 > d\).
  • Tangent plane condition: Plane \(\vec{r}\cdot\vec{n} = d\) touches sphere \(|\vec{r}-\vec{c}| = a\) iff \(\dfrac{|\vec{c}\cdot\vec{n}-d|}{|\vec{n}|} = a\).
  • Plane section: The intersection of a sphere and a plane is a circle. The section through the centre is called a great circle (same centre and radius as the sphere).
  • Any sphere concentric with \(x^2+y^2+z^2+2ux+2vy+2wz+d=0\): same \(u,v,w\) but different constant.
  • Sphere through 4 non-coplanar points: \[\begin{vmatrix}x^2+y^2+z^2&x&y&z&1\\x_1^2+y_1^2+z_1^2&x_1&y_1&z_1&1\\x_2^2+y_2^2+z_2^2&x_2&y_2&z_2&1\\x_3^2+y_3^2+z_3^2&x_3&y_3&z_3&1\\x_4^2+y_4^2+z_4^2&x_4&y_4&z_4&1\end{vmatrix}=0\]
