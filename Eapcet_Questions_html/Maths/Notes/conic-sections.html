Conic Sections

Introduction to Conic Sections

A conic is the locus of a point whose distance from a fixed point (focus \(S\)) bears a constant ratio (eccentricity \(e\)) to its distance from a fixed line (directrix \(l\)).

  • If \(0 < e < 1\) → conic is an Ellipse
  • If \(e = 1\) → conic is a Parabola
  • If \(e > 1\) → conic is a Hyperbola
General Equation of a Conic

If fixed point is \((x_1, y_1)\) and fixed line is \(ax+by+c=0\), the conic is:

\[(a^2+b^2)\left[(x-x_1)^2+(y-y_1)^2\right] = e^2(ax+by+c)^2\]

Simplified: \(ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0\). The second degree equation represents:

ConditionConic
\(\Delta = 0\)A pair of straight lines
\(\Delta = 0,\; h^2 = ab\)Pair of parallel/coincident lines
\(\Delta = 0,\; a+b = 0\)Pair of perpendicular lines
\(\Delta = 0,\; h^2 < ab\)A point
\(a = b \neq 0,\; h = 0,\; \Delta \neq 0\)Circle
\(h^2 = ab,\; \Delta \neq 0\)Parabola
\(h^2 < ab,\; \Delta \neq 0\)Ellipse
\(h^2 > ab,\; \Delta \neq 0\)Hyperbola
\(h^2 > ab,\; a+b = 0,\; \Delta \neq 0\)Rectangular Hyperbola
Important Terms
  • Axis: Line perpendicular to the directrix passing through the focus.
  • Vertex: Intersection of the conic and the axis.
  • Centre: Point which bisects every chord of the conic passing through it.
  • Focal Chord: Any chord passing through the focus.
  • Double Ordinate: A chord perpendicular to the axis of a conic.
  • Latus Rectum: A double ordinate passing through the focus.
  • Focal Distance: Distance of a point \(P(x,y)\) on the conic from the focus \(S\).

Circle

A circle is the locus of a point which moves in a plane such that its distance from a fixed point (centre) is constant (radius).

Standard & Special Equations
  • Centre \((h,k)\), radius \(a\): \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=a^2\)
  • Centre \((0,0)\): \(x^2+y^2=a^2\)
  • Passes through origin: \(x^2+y^2-2hx-2ky=0\)
  • Touches X-axis: \(x^2+y^2-2hx-2ay+h^2=0\)
  • Touches Y-axis: \(x^2+y^2-2ax-2ky+k^2=0\)
  • Touches both axes: \(x^2+y^2-2ax-2ay+a^2=0\)
  • Through origin, centre on X-axis: \(x^2+y^2-2ax=0\)
  • Through origin, centre on Y-axis: \(x^2+y^2-2ay=0\)
  • Through origin, intercepts \(a,b\): \(x^2+y^2-ax-by=0\)

Diameter form — endpoints \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\):

\[(x-x_1)(x-x_2)+(y-y_1)(y-y_2)=0\]

Three points form:

\[\begin{vmatrix}x^2+y^2 & x & y & 1\\ x_1^2+y_1^2 & x_1 & y_1 & 1\\ x_2^2+y_2^2 & x_2 & y_2 & 1\\ x_3^2+y_3^2 & x_3 & y_3 & 1\end{vmatrix}=0\]

Parametric equations: For \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=a^2\):

\[x = h+a\cos\theta,\quad y = k+a\sin\theta,\quad 0\le\theta\le 2\pi\]
General Equation of a Circle

\(x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0\), where centre \(=(-g,-f)\) and radius \(=\sqrt{g^2+f^2-c}\)

  • \(g^2+f^2-c > 0\) → real circle
  • \(g^2+f^2-c = 0\) → point circle
  • \(g^2+f^2-c < 0\) → imaginary circle

Position of point \((x_1,y_1)\): outside/on/inside circle \(S=0\) according as \(S_1 >,=,< 0\)

X-intercept: \(2\sqrt{g^2-c}\)   Y-intercept: \(2\sqrt{f^2-c}\)

Equation of Tangent

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0\):

\[xx_1+yy_1+g(x+x_1)+f(y+y_1)+c=0\]

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(x^2+y^2=r^2\):

\[xx_1+yy_1=r^2\]

Slope form to \(x^2+y^2=r^2\):

\[y=mx\pm r\sqrt{1+m^2}\]

Parametric form at \((r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta)\):

\[x\cos\theta + y\sin\theta = r\]

Condition of tangency: Line \(lx+my+n=0\) touches \(x^2+y^2=r^2\) iff \(r^2(l^2+m^2)=n^2\).

Equation of Normal

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0\):

\[(y_1+f)x-(x_1+g)y+(gy_1-fx_1)=0\]

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(x^2+y^2=r^2\):

\[\frac{x}{x_1}=\frac{y}{y_1}\]
Chord, Pair of Tangents & Pole–Polar

Chord bisected at \((x_1,y_1)\): \(T = S_1\)

Pair of tangents from \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(x^2+y^2=r^2\):

\[SS_1 = T^2\]

Length of tangent from \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0\) is \(\sqrt{S_1}\).

Chord of contact from \((x_1,y_1)\):

\[xx_1+yy_1=r^2 \quad (T=0)\]

Polar of point \((x_1,y_1)\) w.r.t. \(x^2+y^2=r^2\):   \(xx_1+yy_1=r^2\)

Director circle of \(x^2+y^2=r^2\):   \(x^2+y^2=2r^2\)

Common Tangents, Radical Axis & Orthogonal Circles

For two circles with centres \(C_1,C_2\) and radii \(r_1, r_2\):

ConditionPositionCommon Tangents
\(C_1C_2 > r_1+r_2\)Separate4
\(C_1C_2 = r_1+r_2\)Externally tangent3
\(|r_1-r_2| < C_1C_2 < r_1+r_2\)Intersecting2
\(C_1C_2 = |r_1-r_2|\)Internally tangent1
\(C_1C_2 < |r_1-r_2|\)One inside other0

Length of direct common tangent: \(\sqrt{(C_1C_2)^2-(r_1-r_2)^2}\)

Length of transverse common tangent: \(\sqrt{(C_1C_2)^2-(r_1+r_2)^2}\)

Radical axis of \(S_1=0\) and \(S_2=0\): \(S_1-S_2=0\)

Orthogonal circles condition: \(2g_1g_2+2f_1f_2=c_1+c_2\)

Angle of intersection: \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{r_1^2+r_2^2-d^2}{2r_1r_2}\)

Parabola

A parabola is the locus of a point which moves such that its distance from a fixed point (focus) is always equal to its distance from a fixed straight line (directrix). Eccentricity \(e=1\).

Standard Forms & Related Terms
Term\(y^2=4ax\)\(y^2=-4ax\)\(x^2=4ay\)\(x^2=-4ay\)
Vertex\((0,0)\)\((0,0)\)\((0,0)\)\((0,0)\)
Focus\((a,0)\)\((-a,0)\)\((0,a)\)\((0,-a)\)
Axis\(y=0\)\(y=0\)\(x=0\)\(x=0\)
Directrix\(x+a=0\)\(x-a=0\)\(y+a=0\)\(y-a=0\)
Latus rectum\(4a\)\(4a\)\(4a\)\(4a\)
Eccentricity\(e=1\)\(e=1\)\(e=1\)\(e=1\)
Extremities of LR\((a,\pm 2a)\)\((-a,\pm 2a)\)\((\pm 2a,a)\)\((\pm 2a,-a)\)
Parametric\((at^2,2at)\)\((-at^2,2at)\)\((2at,at^2)\)\((2at,-at^2)\)
Focal distance of \(P(h,k)\)\(h+a\)\(a-h\)\(k+a\)\(a-k\)

Position of point \((x_1,y_1)\): outside/on/inside \(y^2=4ax\) according as \(y_1^2-4ax_1 >,=,< 0\)

Other Forms of Parabola (Vertex at \((h,k)\))
  • Opens right: \((y-k)^2=4a(x-h)\)
  • Opens left: \((y-k)^2=-4a(x-h)\)
  • Opens up: \((x-h)^2=4a(y-k)\)
  • Opens down: \((x-h)^2=-4a(y-k)\)
  • Axis parallel to X-axis: \(x=ay^2+by+c\)
  • Axis parallel to Y-axis: \(y=ax^2+bx+c\)
Chord of Parabola

Chord joining \(P(at_1^2,2at_1)\) and \(Q(at_2^2,2at_2)\):

\[y(t_1+t_2)=2x+2at_1t_2\]

Focal chord: If \(P(at^2,2at)\) is one end, the other end is \(\left(\dfrac{a}{t^2},\dfrac{-2a}{t}\right)\) and \(t_1t_2=-1\).

Length of focal chord with parameters \(t_1,t_2\): \(a(t_2-t_1)^2\)

Latus rectum relation: \(4a=\dfrac{4l_1l_2}{l_1+l_2}\) (where \(l_1,l_2\) are focal segment lengths)

Chord bisected at \((x_1,y_1)\): \(T=S_1\), i.e. \(yy_1-2a(x+x_1)=y_1^2-4ax_1\)

Diameter bisecting chords of slope \(m\): \(y=\dfrac{2a}{m}\)

Equation of Tangent

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(y^2=4ax\):

\[yy_1=2a(x+x_1)\]

Parametric form at \((at^2,2at)\):

\[yt=x+at^2\]

Slope form — tangent of slope \(m\):

\[y=mx+\frac{a}{m}, \quad\text{point of contact: }\left(\frac{a}{m^2},\frac{2a}{m}\right)\]

Condition of tangency: \(y=mx+c\) touches \(y^2=4ax\) iff \(c=\dfrac{a}{m}\)

Intersection of two tangents at \(P(at_1^2,2at_1)\) and \(Q(at_2^2,2at_2)\): point \((at_1t_2,\;a(t_1+t_2))\)

Angle between tangents at \(P(at_1^2,2at_1)\) and \(Q(at_2^2,2at_2)\):

\[\tan\theta=\frac{t_2-t_1}{1+t_1t_2}\]

Pair of tangents from \((x_1,y_1)\): \(SS_1=T^2\)

Chord of contact from \((x_1,y_1)\): \(yy_1=2a(x+x_1)\)

Equation of Normal

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(y^2=4ax\):

\[y-y_1=-\frac{y_1}{2a}(x-x_1)\]

Parametric form at \((at^2,2at)\):

\[y+tx=2at+at^3\]

Slope form — normal of slope \(m\):

\[y=mx-2am-am^3,\quad\text{at point }(am^2,-2am)\]

If normal at \(P(at_1^2,2at_1)\) meets the parabola again at \(Q(at_2^2,2at_2)\), then: \(t_2=-t_1-\dfrac{2}{t_1}\)

Maximum 3 normals can be drawn from a point to a parabola.

Conormal points: sum of ordinates = 0; centroid of triangle formed lies on axis.

Important Results on Parabola
  • The tangent at any point bisects the angle between the focal distance and perpendicular to the directrix.
  • Tangents at extremities of a focal chord intersect at right angles on the directrix.
  • Orthocentre of triangle formed by three tangents lies on the directrix.
  • Area of triangle formed by three points on parabola = twice area of triangle formed by their tangents.
  • Director circle of a parabola = its directrix.
  • Polar of \((x_1,y_1)\) w.r.t. \(y^2=4ax\): \(yy_1=2a(x+x_1)\)
  • If \(y_1,y_2,y_3\) are ordinates of vertices of a triangle inscribed in \(y^2=4ax\), its area \(= \dfrac{1}{8a}|(y_1-y_2)(y_2-y_3)(y_3-y_1)|\)

Ellipse

An ellipse is the locus of a point such that the ratio of its distance from a fixed point (focus) to a fixed line (directrix) is always constant and less than unity (\(0 < e < 1\)).

Horizontal Ellipse \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\)  \((a > b > 0)\)
  • Vertices: \(A(a,0),\; A'(-a,0)\)
  • Foci: \(S(ae,0),\; S'(-ae,0)\)
  • Directrices: \(x=\pm\dfrac{a}{e}\)
  • Major axis: \(2a\); Minor axis: \(2b\)
  • Latus rectum: \(\dfrac{2b^2}{a}\)
  • Eccentricity: \(e=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}} < 1\)
  • Relation: \(b^2=a^2(1-e^2)\)
  • Focal distances of \(P(x,y)\): \(|a-ex|\) and \(|a+ex|\); sum \(=2a\)
  • Distance between foci \(=2ae\); between directrices \(=\dfrac{2a}{e}\)
Vertical Ellipse \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\)  \((b > a > 0)\)
  • Vertices: \(B(0,b),\; B'(0,-b)\)
  • Foci: \(S(0,be),\; S'(0,-be)\)
  • Directrices: \(y=\pm\dfrac{b}{e}\)
  • Major axis: \(2b\); Minor axis: \(2a\)
  • Latus rectum: \(\dfrac{2a^2}{b}\)
  • Eccentricity: \(e=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}} < 1\)
  • Focal distances of \(P(x,y)\): \(|b-ey|\) and \(|b+ey|\); sum \(=2b\)
Parametric Equation & Position of a Point

Parametric: \(x=a\cos\phi,\quad y=b\sin\phi\) — \(\phi\) is the eccentric angle.

Auxiliary circle: \(x^2+y^2=a^2\) (described on major axis as diameter).

Special form with centre \((h,k)\): \(\dfrac{(x-h)^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{(y-k)^2}{b^2}=1\)

Position of \((x_1,y_1)\): outside/on/inside according as \(\dfrac{x_1^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y_1^2}{b^2}-1 >,=,< 0\)

Equation of Tangent

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\):

\[\frac{xx_1}{a^2}+\frac{yy_1}{b^2}=1\]

Parametric form at \((a\cos\theta,b\sin\theta)\):

\[\frac{x\cos\theta}{a}+\frac{y\sin\theta}{b}=1\]

Slope form — tangent of slope \(m\):

\[y=mx\pm\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2},\quad\text{contacts: }\left(\pm\frac{a^2m}{\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2}},\;\mp\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2}}\right)\]

Condition of tangency: \(y=mx+c\) touches ellipse iff \(c^2=a^2m^2+b^2\)

Director circle: \(x^2+y^2=a^2+b^2\)

Pair of tangents from \((x_1,y_1)\): \(SS_1=T^2\)

Chord of contact: \(\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}+\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1\)

Chord bisected at \((x_1,y_1)\): \(T=S_1\)

Equation of Normal

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\):

\[\frac{a^2x}{x_1}-\frac{b^2y}{y_1}=a^2-b^2\]

Parametric form at \((a\cos\theta,b\sin\theta)\):

\[ax\sec\theta - by\csc\theta = a^2-b^2\]

Slope form — normal of slope \(m\):

\[y=mx\pm\frac{m(a^2-b^2)}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2m^2}}\]

Condition: \(y=mx+c\) is normal iff \(c^2=\dfrac{m^2(a^2-b^2)^2}{a^2+b^2m^2}\)

Maximum 4 normals can be drawn from a point to an ellipse.

Diameter, Conjugate Diameters & Key Results

Diameter bisecting chords of slope \(m\): \(y=-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2m}x\)

Conjugate diameters: \(y=m_1x\) and \(y=m_2x\) are conjugate iff \(m_1m_2=-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}\)

For conjugate semi-diameters \(CP\) and \(CD\): \(CP^2+CD^2=a^2+b^2\)

  • Tangent and normal at any point bisect the external and internal angles between focal radii.
  • Polar of \((x_1,y_1)\) w.r.t. ellipse: \(\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}+\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1\)
  • Pole of \(lx+my+n=0\): \(\left(-\dfrac{a^2l}{n},\;-\dfrac{b^2m}{n}\right)\)
  • Locus of mid-point of focal chords: \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=\dfrac{ex}{a}\)

Hyperbola

A hyperbola is the locus of a point such that the ratio of its distance from a fixed point (focus) to a fixed line (directrix) is always constant and greater than unity (\(e > 1\)).

Standard Hyperbola \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\)
  • Centre: \(O(0,0)\)
  • Vertices: \(A(a,0),\; A'(-a,0)\)
  • Foci: \(S(ae,0),\; S'(-ae,0)\)
  • Directrices: \(x=\pm\dfrac{a}{e}\)
  • Transverse axis: \(2a\); Conjugate axis: \(2b\)
  • Latus rectum: \(\dfrac{2b^2}{a}\)
  • Eccentricity: \(e=\sqrt{1+\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}} > 1\); \(\quad b^2=a^2(e^2-1)\)
  • Distance between foci: \(2ae\); between directrices: \(\dfrac{2a}{e}\)
  • Ends of latus rectum: \(\left(\pm ae,\;\pm\dfrac{b^2}{a}\right)\)
  • Focal radii: \(|SP|=|ex_1-a|\), \(|S'P|=|ex_1+a|\); difference \(=2a\)
Conjugate Hyperbola \(-\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\)
  • Vertices: \(A(0,b),\; A'(0,-b)\)
  • Foci: \(S(0,be),\; S'(0,-be)\)
  • Directrices: \(y=\pm\dfrac{b}{e}\)
  • Transverse axis: \(2b\); Conjugate axis: \(2a\)
  • Latus rectum: \(\dfrac{2a^2}{b}\)
  • Eccentricity: \(e=\sqrt{1+\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}}\)
  • Focal radii: \(|SP|=|ey_1-b|\), \(|S'P|=|ey_1+b|\)
Parametric Form & Position of Point

Parametric: \(x=a\sec\theta,\quad y=b\tan\theta\)

Hyperbola with centre \((h,k)\): \(\dfrac{(x-h)^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{(y-k)^2}{b^2}=1\)

Position of \((x_1,y_1)\): outside/on/inside according as \(\dfrac{x_1^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y_1^2}{b^2}-1 <,=,> 0\)

Equation of Tangent

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\):

\[\frac{xx_1}{a^2}-\frac{yy_1}{b^2}=1\]

Parametric form at \((a\sec\theta,b\tan\theta)\):

\[\frac{x\sec\theta}{a}-\frac{y\tan\theta}{b}=1\]

Slope form — tangent of slope \(m\):

\[y=mx\pm\sqrt{a^2m^2-b^2},\quad\text{contacts: }\left(\pm\frac{a^2m}{\sqrt{a^2m^2-b^2}},\;\pm\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{a^2m^2-b^2}}\right)\]

Condition of tangency: \(y=mx+c\) touches hyperbola iff \(c^2=a^2m^2-b^2\)

Director circle: \(x^2+y^2=a^2-b^2\) (exists only when \(a^2>b^2\))

Pair of tangents from \((x_1,y_1)\): \(SS_1=T^2\)

Chord of contact: \(\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}-\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1\)

Chord bisected at \((x_1,y_1)\): \(T=S_1\)

Equation of Normal

Point form at \((x_1,y_1)\):

\[\frac{a^2x}{x_1}+\frac{b^2y}{y_1}=a^2+b^2\]

Parametric form at \((a\sec\theta,b\tan\theta)\):

\[ax\cos\theta+by\cot\theta=a^2+b^2\]

Slope form — normal of slope \(m\):

\[y=mx\mp\frac{m(a^2+b^2)}{\sqrt{a^2-b^2m^2}}\]

Maximum 4 normals can be drawn from a point to a hyperbola.

Asymptotes

An asymptote is a straight line at finite distance from origin, to which the tangent to a curve tends as the point of contact goes to infinity.

Asymptotes of \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\):   \(y=\pm\dfrac{b}{a}x\)   or   \(\dfrac{x}{a}\pm\dfrac{y}{b}=0\)

Combined equation: \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=0\)

  • Asymptotes pass through the centre of the hyperbola.
  • A hyperbola and its conjugate have the same asymptotes.
  • Angle between asymptotes: \(2\tan^{-1}\dfrac{b}{a}=2\sec^{-1}(e)\)
  • Hyperbola \(-\) Asymptotes \(=\) Asymptotes \(-\) Conjugate hyperbola (same constant)
Rectangular Hyperbola

A hyperbola whose asymptotes include a right angle. Eccentricity \(e=\sqrt{2}\) always.

Form \(x^2-y^2=a^2\):

  • Asymptotes: \(x\pm y=0\) (perpendicular)
  • Centre: \((0,0)\); Foci: \((\pm\sqrt{2}a,0)\)
  • Directrices: \(x=\pm\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}\)
  • Latus rectum: \(2a\)
  • Parametric: \(x=a\sec\theta,\;y=a\tan\theta\)

Form \(xy=c^2\):

  • Asymptotes: \(x=0\) and \(y=0\)
  • Centre: \((0,0)\); Foci: \((\sqrt{2}c,\sqrt{2}c),\;(-\sqrt{2}c,-\sqrt{2}c)\)
  • Vertices: \(A(c,c),\;A'(-c,-c)\)
  • Directrices: \(x+y=\pm\sqrt{2}c\)
  • Latus rectum: \(2\sqrt{2}c\)
  • Parametric: \(x=ct,\;y=\dfrac{c}{t}\)

Tangent at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(xy=c^2\):

\[xy_1+yx_1=2c^2 \quad\text{or}\quad \frac{x}{x_1}+\frac{y}{y_1}=2\]

Tangent at \(\left(ct,\dfrac{c}{t}\right)\): \(\dfrac{x}{t}+yt=2c\)

Normal at \((x_1,y_1)\) to \(xy=c^2\): \(xx_1-yy_1=x_1^2-y_1^2\)

Normal at \(\left(ct,\dfrac{c}{t}\right)\): \(xt^3-yt-ct^4+c=0\) (4th degree in \(t\) → max 4 normals)

If a triangle is inscribed in a rectangular hyperbola, its orthocentre lies on the hyperbola.

Diameter & Key Results

Diameter bisecting chords of slope \(m\): \(y=\dfrac{b^2}{a^2m}x\)

Conjugate diameters \(y=m_1x\) and \(y=m_2x\): conjugate iff \(m_1m_2=\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}\)

Chord of \(xy=c^2\) with midpoint \((x_1,y_1)\): \(xy_1+yx_1=2x_1y_1\) (i.e. \(T=S_1\))

Chord joining \(t_1,t_2\) on \(xy=c^2\): \(x+yt_1t_2=c(t_1+t_2)\)

Polar of \((x_1,y_1)\) w.r.t. \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1\): \(\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}-\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1\)

Important Results to Remember

  • For a parabola \(y^2=4ax\): tangent at vertex is \(x=0\); focal chord has \(t_1t_2=-1\).
  • The foot of perpendicular from focus on any tangent to a parabola lies on the tangent at the vertex.
  • For ellipse, the normal bisects the internal angle between focal radii; tangent bisects external angle.
  • If \(SM\) and \(S'M'\) are perpendiculars from foci on any tangent to an ellipse, then \(SM\cdot S'M'=b^2\) and \(M,M'\) lie on the auxiliary circle.
  • Two tangents can be drawn from a point to a parabola/ellipse/hyperbola — real and distinct/coincident/imaginary according as point lies outside/on/inside the conic.
  • Max 3 normals from a point to a parabola; max 4 normals to an ellipse, hyperbola, or \(xy=c^2\).
  • For \(xy=c^2\): if a triangle is inscribed, its orthocentre also lies on the curve.
  • Any straight line parallel to an asymptote of a hyperbola intersects the hyperbola at only one point.
  • Common chord of two circles \(S_1=0,S_2=0\) is given by \(S_1-S_2=0\).
  • Radical axes of three circles (non-collinear centres) taken in pairs are concurrent (radical centre).
  • Pole of \(lx+my+n=0\) w.r.t. \(x^2+y^2=a^2\) is \(\left(-\dfrac{a^2l}{n},-\dfrac{a^2m}{n}\right)\).
