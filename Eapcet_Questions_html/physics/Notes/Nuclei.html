⚛️ Nuclear Basics
Atomic number: Z (protons) Mass number: A = Z + N (nucleons) Nuclear radius: R = R₀A^(1/3), R₀ ≈ 1.1×10⁻¹⁵ m Nuclear density: ρ = 3m/(4πR₀³) (approximately constant for all nuclei) 1 amu = 1.66×10⁻²⁷ kg ≈ 931 MeV

📘 Nuclear Forces, Mass Defect & Binding Energy
  • Nuclear force is very strong, short-range, attractive, and nearly charge independent.
  • ~100 times electrostatic force and ~10³⁸ times gravitational force (order insight).
Mass defect: Δm = [Zm_p + (A-Z)m_n - m_nucleus] Einstein mass-energy: E = mc² Binding energy: E_b = Δm c² Binding energy per nucleon: E_b/A
Nuclei with highest binding energy per nucleon are most stable (around iron region).
☢️ Nuclear Reactions, Q-Value, Fission & Fusion
Q-value of reaction: Q = (mass of reactants - mass of products)c² Q > 0: exoergic, Q < 0: endoergic
ProcessDescriptionKey point
Nuclear fissionHeavy nucleus splits into lighter nucleiLarge energy release; chain reaction possible
Nuclear fusionLight nuclei combine to form heavier nucleusRequires very high temperature (~10⁷ K), source of sun’s energy
Hydrogen bomb is based on fusion; nuclear reactors primarily use controlled fission.

📉 Radioactivity & Decay Law

Radioactivity is spontaneous emission of radiations by unstable nuclei.

Radioactive decay law: -dN/dt = λN N = N₀e^(-λt) Half life: T₁/₂ = ln2/λ = 0.693/λ Mean life: τ = 1/λ Relation: τ = 1.44 T₁/₂ Activity: R = -dN/dt = λN R = R₀e^(-λt)
UnitMeaning
