Physics Handbook — Chapter 23

Magnetism and Matter

Magnetic Dipole · Earth Magnetism · Magnetometers · Magnetic Materials · Hysteresis

🧲 Magnetic Dipole & Magnetic Moment
Magnetic dipole moment: M = m(2l) Direction: S → N SI unit: A·m² = J/T

Magnetic field lines form closed loops; outside magnet N→S and inside S→N.

📘 Magnetic Field due to Dipole
LocationField expression
Axial line\(B=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2Mr}{(r^2-l^2)^2}\), for \(r\gg l\): \(B\approx\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2M}{r^3}\)
Equatorial line\(B=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{M}{(r^2+l^2)^{3/2}}\), for \(r\gg l\): \(B\approx\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{M}{r^3}\)
General angle θDepends on axial and equatorial components; \(B\propto 1/r^3\) for far field
Torque on dipole in uniform field: τ = MB sinθ Potential energy: U = -MB cosθ = -M·B
⚛️ Magnetic Moment of Current Loop & Atom
Current loop: M = IA For N turns: M = NIA Atomic orbital magnetic moment: M = (e/2mₑ)L Bohr magneton: μ_B = eh/(4πmₑ) ≈ 9.27×10⁻²⁴ A·m²
🌍 Earth’s Magnetism
QuantityDefinition/Relation
Declination (θ)Angle between magnetic meridian and geographic meridian
Inclination or dip (δ)Angle made by earth’s magnetic field with horizontal
Horizontal component\(H=B\cos\delta\)
Vertical component\(V=B\sin\delta\)
Resultant field\(B=\sqrt{H^2+V^2}\), \(\tan\delta=V/H\)
Magnetic latitude λ relation: tanδ = 2tanλ Total intensity: I = I₀√(1+3sin²λ), where I₀ = M/R³
🗺️ Magnetic Maps, Neutral Points & Instruments
  • Isogonic line: equal declination
  • Agonic line: zero declination
  • Isoclinic line: equal dip
  • Aclinic line: zero dip
  • Isodynamic line: equal H
Neutral points: When bar magnet N towards Earth S → neutral points on axial line. When bar magnet N towards Earth N → neutral points on equatorial line.
Tangent law: tanθ = B₁/B₂ Deflection magnetometer: Used to determine M and H. Vibration magnetometer: T = 2π√(I/MB) ⇒ B = 4π²I/(MT²) Tangent galvanometer: I = (2RH tanθ)/(μ₀N)
🧪 Magnetic Materials & Hysteresis
TypeBehaviorExamples
DiamagneticWeakly repelled; χm negativeBi, Cu, Ag, Au
ParamagneticWeakly attracted; χm positive smallAl, Pt, O₂
FerromagneticStrongly attracted; large χmFe, Co, Ni
Hysteresis loop terms: Retentivity: residual magnetism at H=0 Coercivity: reverse field to reduce magnetism to zero Hysteresis loss = area of B-H loop
