Physics Handbook — Chapter 24

Electromagnetic Induction

Faraday’s Law · Lenz’s Law · Motional emf · Eddy Currents · Self & Mutual Induction

⚙️ Basics & Magnetic Flux

When magnetic flux linked with an electric circuit changes, an emf is induced. This is electromagnetic induction.

Magnetic flux: Φ = ∫ B·dS = B S cosθ SI unit: weber (Wb) = T·m² CGS unit: maxwell 1 weber = 10⁸ maxwell Dimension: [M L² T⁻² A⁻¹]
📘 Faraday’s Laws & Lenz’s Law
Faraday’s law: e = -dΦ/dt Induced current: I = e/R = -(1/R)(dΦ/dt) Induced charge: q = (1/R)ΔΦ
Negative sign indicates Lenz’s law: induced current always opposes the cause producing it (conservation of energy).
For rotating coil in uniform B: I = I₀ sin(ωt) I₀ = NBAω/R where N=turns, A=area, B=field, ω=angular speed
🧲 Direction of Induced Current & Motional emf
  • If flux through loop increases → induced current creates opposite field.
  • If flux decreases → induced current supports existing field.
  • Direction by Fleming’s right-hand rule: thumb (motion), forefinger (B), middle finger (induced current).
Motional emf for rod: e = B l v (for v ⟂ B) General case: e = B l v sinθ Rotating rod/disc in uniform B: e = (1/2)Bωl² = BAf (where A = swept area, f = frequency)
If a rectangular coil translates with constant velocity in uniform magnetic field, flux remains constant and induced emf is zero.
🔁 Eddy Currents, Self Induction & LR Energy
Eddy currents: I_eddy = e/R = -(1/R)(dΦ/dt) Reduced by laminated cores.

Eddy currents cause heating and power loss (Foucault currents).

Self-induction: Φ = LI e = -L(dI/dt) Unit of L: henry (H) Dimension: [M L² T⁻² A⁻²]
Self inductance formulas: Long solenoid: L = μ₀N²A/l = μ₀n²Al With magnetic core: L = μ₀μrN²A/l Toroid: L = μ₀N²A/(2πr) Energy stored in inductor: U = (1/2)LI²
🔄 Mutual Induction, Coupling & Coil Grouping
Mutual induction: Φ = MI e = -M(dI/dt) Mutual inductance of coaxial long solenoids: M = μ₀N₁N₂A/l = μ₀n₁n₂Al Coefficient of coupling: K = M/√(L₁L₂) K max = 1 (coaxial), minimum when coils at right angles
CombinationEquivalent Inductance
Series, uncoupled (K=0)L = L₁ + L₂ + L₃ + ...
Parallel, uncoupled (K=0)1/L = 1/L₁ + 1/L₂ + 1/L₃ + ...
Series, coupled (same current direction)L = L₁ + L₂ + 2M
Series, coupled (opposite current direction)L = L₁ + L₂ − 2M
Parallel, coupled (same direction)L = (L₁L₂ − M²)/(L₁ + L₂ + 2M)
Parallel, coupled (opposite direction)L = (L₁L₂ − M²)/(L₁ + L₂ − 2M)
