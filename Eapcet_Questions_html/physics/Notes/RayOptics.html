Ray Optics and Optical Instruments

Mirrors · Refraction · Lenses · Prism · Human Eye · Microscope · Telescope

💡 Light & Reflection Basics
Speed of light in vacuum: c = 3×10⁸ m/s Light frequency remains unchanged across media; speed and wavelength change. Laws of reflection: 1) Incident ray, reflected ray and normal are coplanar. 2) i = r

Regular reflection occurs from smooth surfaces, diffused reflection from rough surfaces.

🪞 Mirrors (Plane + Spherical)
Mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u Relation: f = R/2 Power of mirror: P = 1/f (f in m) Magnification: m = h_i/h_o = -v/u Areal magnification = m² Axial magnification = -(v²/u²)
Plane mirror: image virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted. Focal length and radius are infinite.
🌈 Refraction, Snell’s Law & TIR
Snell’s law: μ = sin i / sin r Refractive index: μ = c/v Critical angle C: sin C = 1/μ (for denser to rarer) Condition for total internal reflection: 1) Light from denser to rarer medium 2) i > C

Optical fibre works on repeated total internal reflection.

🔍 Lenses
Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u Lens maker’s formula (in air): 1/f = (μ-1)(1/R₁ - 1/R₂) Power of lens: P = 1/f (dioptre) P_combination = P₁ + P₂ + ... Magnification: m = v/u = h_i/h_o

🔺 Prism, Dispersion & Deviation
Prism relation: μ = sin[(A + δm)/2] / sin(A/2) For small-angle prism: δ = (μ - 1)A Angular dispersion: Δδ = δ_v - δ_r Dispersive power: ω = (μ_v - μ_r)/(μ_y - 1)
At minimum deviation condition, angle of incidence equals angle of emergence.
👁️ Human Eye & Defects
  • Normal eye range: 25 cm to ∞.
  • Myopia corrected by concave lens.
  • Hypermetropia corrected by convex lens.
  • Presbyopia due to reduced accommodation with age.
  • Astigmatism corrected by cylindrical lens.
  • Colour blindness: defective cone cells.
Power required for correction: P = 1/f (dioptre) Near point (normal): 25 cm
