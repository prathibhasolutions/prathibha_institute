Atomic Physics

Atomic physics deals with the structure of atoms and the interaction of electrons with radiation.

Bohr’s Atomic Model

Bohr proposed a model for hydrogen atom based on quantum theory.

Postulates
  • Electrons revolve around nucleus in circular orbits.
  • These orbits are called stationary states.
  • Angular momentum of electron is quantized.

\[ mvr = \frac{nh}{2\pi} \]

Where

  • n = 1,2,3,4… (principal quantum number)
  • h = Planck’s constant
Radius, Velocity and Energy of Electron (Bohr Atom)

For hydrogen-like atoms (atomic number \(Z\)), the radius, velocity and energy of electron in the \(n^{th}\) orbit are given by:

Radius of nth Orbit

\[ r_n = 0.53\frac{n^2}{Z}\; \text{Å} \]

Where \(n\) = principal quantum number \(Z\) = atomic number

Velocity of Electron

\[ v_n = 2.18 \times 10^6 \frac{Z}{n}\; \text{m/s} \]

Energy of Electron

\[ E_n = -13.6 \frac{Z^2}{n^2}\; \text{eV} \]

Negative sign indicates that electron is bound to nucleus.

Important Points for EAPCET
  • Radius increases as \(n^2\).
  • Velocity decreases as \(1/n\).
  • Energy varies as \(1/n^2\).
  • For hydrogen atom \(Z=1\).
de-Broglie Explanation of Bohr’s Second Postulate

Electron behaves like a matter wave.

\[ \lambda = \frac{h}{mv} \]

For stable orbit

\[ 2\pi r = n\lambda \]

This condition leads to Bohr quantization condition.

Hydrogen Spectrum

Hydrogen atom emits spectral lines when electron jumps between energy levels.

\[ \frac{1}{\lambda} = R \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right) \]

Where

  • R = Rydberg constant
  • \(R = 1.097\times10^7 m^{-1}\)
Hydrogen Spectrum Series
  • Lyman Series
    Electron transition: \(n = 2,3,4...\rightarrow n=1\)
    Region: Ultraviolet

  • Balmer Series
    Electron transition: \(n = 3,4,5...\rightarrow n=2\)
    Region: Visible

  • Paschen Series
    Electron transition: \(n = 4,5,6...\rightarrow n=3\)
    Region: Infrared

  • Brackett Series
    Electron transition: \(n = 5,6,7...\rightarrow n=4\)
    Region: Infrared

  • Pfund Series
    Electron transition: \(n = 6,7,8...\rightarrow n=5\)
    Region: Infrared
