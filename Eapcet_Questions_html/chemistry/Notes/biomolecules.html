Biomolecules

Biomolecules are organic compounds present in living organisms that perform essential biological functions.

  • Carbohydrates
  • Proteins
  • Lipids
  • Nucleic acids
Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are polyhydroxy aldehydes or ketones or compounds that produce them on hydrolysis.

\[ C_x(H_2O)_y \]

Classification

  • Monosaccharides
  • Oligosaccharides
  • Polysaccharides
Monosaccharides
Glucose
  • Molecular formula \(C_6H_{12}O_6\)
  • Also called dextrose
  • Reducing sugar
  • Exists in cyclic and open chain form
  • Main source of energy for body
Fructose
  • Ketose sugar
  • Also called fruit sugar
  • Sweetest naturally occurring sugar
  • Reducing sugar
Oligosaccharides

Oligosaccharides yield 2–10 monosaccharide units on hydrolysis.

Disaccharide Components Type Source
Sucrose Glucose + Fructose Non-reducing sugar Table sugar
Lactose Glucose + Galactose Reducing sugar Milk sugar
Maltose Glucose + Glucose Reducing sugar Product of starch hydrolysis
